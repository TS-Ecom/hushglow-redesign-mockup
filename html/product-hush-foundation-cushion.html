A cushion foundation is a lightweight cream foundation stored in a compact. It applies in thin, breathable layers that blend effortlessly into the skin for a natural, even finish — perfect for comfortable, everyday wear.

Along with beautifying coverage, this formula provides SPF 30 protection to help defend your skin from daily sun exposure. It is the perfect way to combine complexion-perfecting makeup with everyday protection in one easy step.

What it is. Hush Glow Foundation Cushion is a lightweight, breathable cream foundation made to feel like skincare. It's perfect for women who want smooth, glowing skin — without the heavy, cakey feeling of traditional makeup.

Why it's special. This cushion is more than just coverage. It blends into your skin tone, hydrates dry areas, and smooths over fine lines — leaving your skin looking fresh and feeling comfortable all day.

Step 1 – Prep Your Skin
Start with clean, moisturized skin. You don't need a primer — just your usual skincare is enough.

Step 2 – Press the Sponge
Gently press the mushroom applicator into the cushion. A little goes a long way, so no need to press hard or overdo it.

Step 3 – Tap, Don't Rub
Use light tapping motions to apply the product to your face. Start in the center and blend outward.

Step 4 – Build If You Need More
One layer gives a soft, natural glow. For more coverage, just tap again where needed — without it feeling heavy.
