Makeup Artist Tips

For Under-Eyes:

Dot three small points along the under-eye area — inner corner, center, outer corner. Blend outward with the built-in brush using light tapping motions, never dragging.

For Spots & Redness:

Swipe directly onto the area and pat gently with the brush until it melts in. Layer for extra coverage without ever looking heavy.

Pro Tips

Apply after your moisturizer has fully absorbed — this helps the formula glide instead of sliding around.
