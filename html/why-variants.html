“Why Hush Glow?” — five treatments

All five keep the construction that is on the store today: the copy on the left, the table on the right, and our column carried by a band that runs behind it and overshoots it top and bottom. What changes is the colour of that band and how the two marks are drawn. Switch to the phone width to see how each one folds.

Width
Option 1The live block, built properly

Same design as today. The only change is that the band is one shape running the full height behind our column, so it reads as holding the column rather than as a rectangle parked above the table. Both headers sit on the same line.

Option 2Ink band

Same geometry, band in the brand’s black with the wordmark reversed out and the ticks in sand. The beige band currently sits very close to the section fill, so the column barely reads as ours; black makes the ownership unmistakable.

Option 3Band plus a tinted pair

On paper rather than grey, with a cream band and both columns lightly tinted — ours warm, theirs cool. The two read as a pair being compared instead of one column with a decoration behind it.

Option 4Lifted card, no band

The whole section moves onto cream and our column becomes a white card that lifts off it, wordmark included. Nothing is tinted, the shadow does the work — the quietest of the five and the most in line with the rest of the page.

Option 5Brand marks, left-aligned rows

Live geometry kept, but the stock green tick and hard black cross are replaced by a burgundy tick and a soft grey cross, and the claims are set flush left instead of centred so the eye runs down them. The smallest change of the five, the biggest gain in polish.
